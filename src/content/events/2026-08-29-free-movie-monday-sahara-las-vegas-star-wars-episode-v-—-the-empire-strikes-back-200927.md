Enjoy a free outdoor movie night at The Pool at SAHARA Las Vegas. Free Movie Mondays feature family-friendly films on a massive outdoor LED screen, with swimming available during the screening. Doors open at 7:30 PM and the film begins at 8:00 PM. Complimentary lounge chairs are available on a first-come, first-served basis, with towels provided. Popcorn, snacks, and beverages are available for purchase at the poolside bar. The series runs every Monday through September 7, 2026.
