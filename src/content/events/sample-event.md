The Las Vegas Farmers Market at Downtown Summerlin takes place every Saturday from 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM under The Pavilion. Shop fresh fruits and vegetables from the Southwest, authentic European-style breads and cakes, local breads and pastries, citrus, leafy greens, nuts, pasta, salad mixes, fresh-cut flowers, bedding plants, avocados, squash, pomelos, bok choy, strawberries, and more. A great weekly stop for fresh produce, specialty foods, and locally made goods.
